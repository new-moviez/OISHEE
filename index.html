The Biography

Beauty crowned by the nation,
stories told on screen.

Jannatul Ferdous Oishee rose to national prominence when she was crowned Miss World Bangladesh 2018, representing her country on the global Miss World stage in Sanya, China. From pageant halls to studio sets, she carried that same poise into cinema - earning leading and featured roles across Bangladesh's biggest contemporary releases.

On screen she is best known as the sharp, magnetic Sylvi in the hit action franchises Mission Extreme and Black War: Mission Extreme 2, while her performances in Raat Jaga Phool, Noor and Adam reveal a range that moves effortlessly between vulnerability and steel. Off screen, she is a red-carpet mainstay and sought-after face for luxury fashion and beauty campaigns.
